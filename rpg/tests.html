Hollow Crypt tests

Unit tests for the game's pure logic, mirrored from index.html, plus a manual checklist.

Manual checklist

  • Graphics stay sharp at any window size and on high-DPI screens (no blurry pixels or text).
  • Title screen shows Continue only when a save exists.
  • Camp chest gives 2 bandages and 2 wood; the Camp Warden (Talk) explains the controls.
  • E chops trees (wood), mines grey rocks (stone), picks bushes (herb); iron and coal say a pickaxe is needed until you have one.
  • Crafting: Wooden Sword (4 wood) auto-equips; Roast Meat and iron recipes say "Needs the camp fire" away from camp and work next to it.
  • J / Space swings the equipped weapon; enemies flash, get knocked back, show damage numbers and drop loot.
  • K fires arrows when you have a bow and arrows; the arrow count drops.
  • Q uses roast meat, a bandage or raw meat; hearts refill.
  • Taking a hit flashes the hero, knocks them back and removes hearts; a shield shows "blocked" on strong hits.
  • The road leads to the crypt stairs in the north-east mountains; walking onto the stairs fades into the dungeon.
  • The crypt is dark: wall torches and your own light show the way; carrying a torch makes your light larger.
  • The sealed door stays locked until you take the key from the east wing chest.
  • All world art comes from rpg/assets (fortress kit, hunt animals, rocks); boars, foxes, hares, deer and grouse animate in 4 directions.
  • The Hollow King wakes when you enter his room: slam (red circle telegraph), spinning knife volleys, spearman summons below half health.
  • Defeating him drops the Hollow Crown, gold and a Heart Crystal (+1 heart) and shows the victory screen.
  • Dying shows "You fell"; Enter wakes you at camp with full health and your gear.
  • Resting at the camp fire heals and saves; reloading the page and choosing Continue restores everything.
  • Esc pauses and lists all controls; X toggles sound.
