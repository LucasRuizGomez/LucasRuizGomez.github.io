Manual checklist

  • Study: Space/Enter flips, 1–4 grade, Space on the back = Good; each button shows the next interval.
  • Phone: tap the card to flip; buttons are pinned to the bottom above the home indicator; swipe left = Again, right = Good.
  • 🔊 reads the word and the example with a German voice (needs a de-DE voice installed on the device).
  • Reverse card (English → Deutsch) appears only after the German → English card reaches review.
  • "Fertig für heute!" shows when the next card is due; Extra practice doesn't change the counts.
  • Deck tab: sort by word / status / ✓✗, search, click a row to edit, move to another deck, delete asks first.
  • Decks tab: create "Redemittel C1", add cards by hand, select it in the header and study it alone; "All decks" mixes both.
  • Deck delete asks for the deck name; archive hides it from the selector.
  • Add words (needs Supabase): paste Zorns, fordert, verrappeln, Urteil → Zorn, fordern normalized; verrappeln → sich aufrappeln (corrected); Urteil marked duplicate and unticked.
  • Export a deck, import it into a new deck: same counts and progress.
  • Answer cards in airplane mode: "N answers waiting to sync" appears and clears when back online; the other device shows the same progress after a refresh.
  • Install: "Add to Home Screen" opens full screen with the icon; app shell loads offline.
  • Light and dark mode follow the system.
